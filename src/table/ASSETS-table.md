## Photo-based content edits

The visible site uses `dining-room-edited.png` and `seasonal-plate-edited.png`, created with the built-in image generator from the two licensed source photographs above, at the user's request. These are edited images, not unmodified photographs by the original photographers.

The room edit preserves the timber interior, composition, grain, daylight and warm lamps, while removing people and changing seat cushions, table flowers and wall art. The food edit preserves the overhead camera, natural lighting, hand, plate and table, while changing the contents to a beet, chicory, goat-cheese and orange salad. No reference-restaurant assets are used.
